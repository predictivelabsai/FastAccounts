# Integration logo notices

- QuickBooks and Xero SVG paths are sourced from the Simple Icons project
  (`simple-icons/simple-icons`) and coloured with their catalogue brand colours.
- The Merit Aktiva SVG is copied from Merit Tarkvara's official website.
  These three official files are only minified; their artwork is unchanged.
- HMRC, Estonian Tax and Customs Board (EMTA) and SmartAccounts tiles are
  neutral FastAccounts-created text identifiers, not reproductions of
  government crests or provider logos.
- The HR software catalogue tiles are neutral FastAccounts-created placeholder
  initials, not official provider logos or reproductions of brand artwork.
  They use a neutral grey tile so they never borrow the FastAccounts violet.
- FastHR is a sibling FastSME product and uses an ink tile.
- Open banking, employee file import and e-invoice tiles are FastAccounts-created
  icons from the FastAccounts line icon set on a violet tile.
- Lettering on the created tiles is Bricolage Grotesque Bold (SIL Open Font
  Licence 1.1) converted to outlines, so it renders the same everywhere.

All product names and trademarks belong to their respective owners. Their use
identifies planned interoperability and does not imply endorsement or a live
partnership.
